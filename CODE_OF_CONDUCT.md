# Code of Conduct

Open Battle is a place to build and play games together. Be the opponent people want to play again.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the code or to wargaming.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Assume good faith.
- Credit other people's work, including rules ideas and house rules.
- Respect "no": to a game, a rematch, a voice chat, or a request.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or slurs, in any project space or at an open table.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, or content that is sexual or violent beyond what the games themselves depict.
- Threats, doxxing, or sharing someone's private information.
- Cheating or manipulating ranked results, and pressuring people to sign results.
- Deliberately posting publisher IP to put the project at risk.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues and discussions, the public open-tables and ranked boards, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Email the lead maintainer listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md), or open a private security advisory on GitHub if it involves an exploit. In the app, **Report** on an open table or player hides them for you. Reports are kept private. Maintainers may warn, remove content, or ban someone from project spaces.

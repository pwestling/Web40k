# Brinewatch

A skirmish game for two players, about an hour long, played with a crew of five to ten models each. At low tide the drowned harbour town of Brinewatch comes up out of the sea, and three crews climb its broken towers for what the water left.

Brinewatch is Open Battle's second game of its own. Everything in it (names, rules, stats, missions, the campaign, the starter table and the stand-in figures) is original, so it can be played, shown and shared freely. It is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): credit "Brinewatch, Open Battle contributors". The whole game is one file, [brinewatch.js](brinewatch.js), a workshop package; [WORKSHOP.md](WORKSHOP.md) is what writing it needed from the SDK and the engine.

It plays differently from Rift Lanterns: each model is its own unit and takes its own **go** of two **action points**, ready and expended model by model; a model can **stand guard** and shoot the first enemy that moves or acts in its sight; ruins have floors to climb for **vantage**; each crew's **Lurker** hides in a hideout picked in secret; and three games make a **campaign** with experience, honours and scars.

**To play:** press **Play now** under Brinewatch on Open Battle's start page, or **Play the computer**. To play a friend, host a game and pick Brinewatch in the Game list. For a campaign, start one under **Campaign** and play the three missions in order.

## The rules

**[RULES.md](RULES.md)** has the whole game: rounds and goes, the actions, shooting, fighting, guards, lurkers, the campaign, the three crews, the three missions, the starter table ([table.svg](table.svg)) and a quick reference. It is made from the module by `pnpm rulebook`, so it always says what the game plays; the app shows the same page under **Rules, print and play** on the Brinewatch card. What the app checks and how, rule by rule, is in [docs/rules-coverage/brinewatch.md](../../docs/rules-coverage/brinewatch.md).

## Print and play

Press **Print and play** on the Brinewatch rules page. It makes a PDF in the browser: the rules sheet, a card for each model, marker and wound tokens, a quick-reference card, and cut-out stand-in figures with bases at true size. Open Battle on a phone (**At a real table**) can keep the score while you play: it asks what it can't measure.

# Rules coverage: Brinewatch

- **Source:** Brinewatch's own rules page, [games/brinewatch/RULES.md](../../games/brinewatch/RULES.md), made by `pnpm rulebook` from the module (version 1.0.0, CC BY 4.0). Sections: Rounds and goes, Actions, Shooting, Fighting, Standing guard, Hidden lurkers, Holding a marker, The campaign, The crews, Missions, The starter table, Quick reference.
- **Code:** [games/brinewatch/brinewatch.js](../../games/brinewatch/brinewatch.js), plus the shared engine where a row says "core".
- **Date:** 2026-10-09 (#69, written with the game).
- **Tests:** `A` is `src/sandbox/brinewatch.audit.test.ts`, `AP` is `src/sandbox/actionPoints.test.ts`, `R` is `src/printplay/rulebook.test.ts`. The soak (`src/soak/brinewatch.soak.test.ts`, `SOAK=1`) tags each rule below that comes up in play and requires most of them in every run.
- **At a real table** (the phone companion) nothing is measured. Shoot asks what the board would measure (the `told` questions: in sight and Range, vantage, cover, obscured) and Fight asks for contact; guards, lurkers' hideouts and the moved hook do nothing at a real table (`atTable`), where the players keep them. Those are player-confirmed by design and aren't counted as separate rows.

**Engine and SDK work this game needed** (#69), each generic and documented in [docs/packages.md](../packages.md):

1. Action points in plain activations: a slot's `actionsPerActivation` is a go's points, `CodeAction.cost` what an action spends, and a go lasts while any are left (core turn.ts, reducer.ts, script.ts).
2. The `moved` turn hook, after a unit's move in the battle (core script.ts).
3. A go ends when its unit is taken out in the middle of it (core reducer.ts).
4. The computer players: a unit in reserve can take its code actions (Emerge), models climb floors (`BotTuning.climbs`), and a game's `evaluate` gets a GameView for sight and cover (src/bot, src/soak/bot.ts).
5. The rulebook and print and play take a game's own words and terrain colours, and models' roles and abilities (`Rulebook.words`, `terrainColors`; src/printplay).
6. The review benchmark benches a whole game from a package (src/review/bench/build.ts `scene`'s `systemPkg`).

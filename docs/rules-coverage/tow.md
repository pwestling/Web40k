# Rules coverage: Warhammer: The Old World (`tow-hand`)

- **Source:** the Warhammer: The Old World core rulebook (Games Workshop, 2024), by section name, from the auditor's own knowledge of it: the turn sequence; the Strategy phase (start of turn, Command, Conjuration, Rally Fleeing Troops); the Movement phase (declare charges and charge reactions, compulsory moves, charge moves, failed charges, remaining moves); the Shooting phase; the Combat phase; Psychology; Leadership; characters; magic; troop types; special rules; terrain. Where the code says it was checked against the community rules index (tow.whfb.app, 2026-10-07/08, see `research/old-world-gaps.md`), that check stands and is noted. Anything only from general knowledge is marked **unverified**.
- **Audited:** 2026-10-08 (issue #55).
- **Code:** `src/systems/tow/` (system.ts, module.ts, combat.ts, psychology.ts, reminders.ts, ranks.ts, troops.ts, specialRules.ts, characters.ts, magic.ts, items.ts, terrainTests.ts). Movement measuring, the Charge panel and the regiment panel are shared UI (`src/ui/ChargePanel.tsx`, `src/ui/RegimentPanel.tsx`) driven by this module's constants.
- **Tests:** **C** = `src/systems/tow/combat.test.ts`, **M** = `magic.test.ts`, **S** = `shooting.test.ts`, **R** = `ranks.test.ts`, **V** = `cover.test.ts`, **T** = `terrainTests.test.ts`, **CH** = `characters.test.ts`, **I** = `items.test.ts`, **A** = `audit.test.ts` (describe "Old World rules audit (#55)"), **U** = `universal.test.ts` (describe "Old World universal special rules (#66)"), all in `src/systems/tow/`.
- Names and our own paraphrase only. No rules text, profiles or points.

Status key: **automated** (the app does it), **advisory check** (the app measures, flags or offers it; the player decides), **manual reminder** (the app prompts at the right time; the player resolves it), **missing** (nothing).

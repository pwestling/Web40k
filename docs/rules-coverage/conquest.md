# Rules coverage: Conquest: The Last Argument of Kings (`conquest-hand`)

- **Source:** `research/conquest-rules.md` (2026-10-07), the engine team's paraphrased notes on the Conquest 2.0 core rules ("2026 Post-Adepticon" PDF, para-bellum.com). Sections: Characteristics, Stands, Round, Actions, Combat, Special rules, Scenarios, Lists. Points the notes mark [secondary] come from Order of Gamers v3 or the tournament pack. Core-rules knowledge filled in only where the notes are thin, and is flagged as such.
- **Audited:** 2026-10-08 (issue #55).
- **Code:** `src/systems/conquest/` (system.ts, command.ts, charge.ts, march.ts, morale.ts, reinforce.ts, characters.ts, module.ts, CommandPanel.tsx).
- **Tests:** **C** = `src/systems/conquest/conquest.test.ts`, **A** = `src/systems/conquest/audit.test.ts` (describe "Conquest rules audit").
- Names and our own paraphrase only. No rules text, profiles or points.

Status key: **automated** (the app does it), **advisory check** (the app flags it or measures it; the player decides), **manual reminder** (the app prompts at the right time; the player resolves it), **missing** (nothing).

## Summary

| Status          | Rows |
| --------------- | ---- |

| advisory check  | 11   |

**Known wrong**

- None open. Supremacy was fixed in the core (#55).

**Fixed in this audit**

- Inspired gave +1 Clash at any Clash. It now stops at Clash 3, with a reminder above that, and does nothing for a Broken regiment.
- Shield raised the final defense target, so it also raised Evasion. It now adds to D only.
- Engaged regiments could March, Charge, Volley, Take Aim, Rally and Reform. Unengaged ones could Inspire and Withdraw. Both are now gated on engagement, and the test that charged from contact now charges from 3" away.
- Volley had no half-range bonus. It now adds +1 shot per stand.
- New: engaged-only Combat Rally and Combat Reform buttons, plus reminders for obscured volleys and for failed charges (on the Charge hint; automated since #57).

**Closed in #57**

- Re-roll one face: `reroll: { values: [6] }` re-rolls named natural results (runner.ts, odds.ts), so Inspired at Clash 4+ re-rolls natural 6s.
- Charge outcome: core `TurnHooks.charge` runs after a charge roll or a charge move. Conquest's hook (charge.ts) makes a short charge lose Inspired and end the activation, and a landed one Inspired.
- Charge targeting: the charge's targets are enemies in the front arc and in sight (`chargeable`), with a reason when there are none. Picking one in the charge panel is UI work (UX 396).
- March limits: advisory warnings for half rate sideways or backwards and for ending within 1" of an enemy (march.ts).
- Casualty order: the allocate step's `last` holds a named command stand back until last.
- Volley counts only front-rank stands with a clear shot.

**Still missing:** stand Size and models per stand, the command stand's place in the front rank, objective zones and seize values, the Warlord and how many regiments a character leads, and importing from the official builder's text format.
